Implemented 2025 research constants and guarded assumptions:
- Educator expense maximum: $300 per eligible educator; two eligible educators
  on a joint return are calculated separately.
- Traditional IRA base limit: $7,000 per owner, plus $1,000 when age 50 or older.
- Active-participant IRA deduction phaseouts: $79,000–$89,000 for Single/HOH,
  $126,000–$146,000 for MFJ, and $0–$10,000 for MFS. The MFJ nonparticipant
  spouse of an active participant uses $236,000–$246,000.
- Student-loan interest maximum: $2,500; phaseout $85,000–$100,000 other than
  joint/MFS, $170,000–$200,000 joint; MFS is ineligible.
- HSA contribution limits: self-only $4,300, family $8,550, with $1,000 age-55
  catch-up. The total limit is month-prorated unless the reviewed last-month
  rule applies. Nonqualified distributions use the 20 percent additional-tax
  rule unless a reviewed death, disability, or age-65 exception applies.
- Defined-contribution limit used as a hard upper guard: $70,000. The supported
  self-employed SEP deduction uses the self-employed 20-percent rate and a
  reviewed activity/limit; SIMPLE and qualified-plan calculations block until
  plan-specific inputs are implemented.
- Premium-tax-credit applicable percentages are represented by the 2025
  research interpolation table, with explicit Form 1095-A, household, SLCSP,
  shared-policy, below-poverty-exception, and MFS blockers. Repayment limitation bands are
  $375/$750, $975/$1,950, and $1,625/$3,250 before the 400-percent band.
- Social Security wage base: $176,100; employee maximum withholding $10,918.20.
- Form 6251 exemption amounts: $88,100 for Single/HOH, $137,000 for MFJ/QSS,
  and $68,500 for MFS. Phaseout begins at $626,350 except $1,252,700 for
  MFJ/QSS. The 26-percent rate breakpoint is $239,100, or $119,550 for MFS.
  The supported slice adds back the standard deduction or reviewed itemized
  taxes, accepts enumerated nonnegative preference adjustments, applies an
  AMT-specific QBI limitation and the preferential-income computation, and
  blocks dependent-child worksheets, Form 8801 and unrepresented adjustments.
