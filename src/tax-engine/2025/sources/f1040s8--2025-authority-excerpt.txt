Part I: multiply qualifying children under age 17 with the required SSN by $2,200; multiply other dependents by $500; use a phaseout threshold of $400,000 for married filing jointly and $200,000 for all other statuses; round excess modified AGI up to the next $1,000 and multiply by 5%.

Part II-A: the per-child refundable limit is $1,700. Earned income above $2,500 is multiplied by 15%.

Part II-B for three or more qualifying children: add withheld Social Security, Medicare and Additional Medicare taxes to the specified Schedule 1 and Schedule 2 taxes, subtract Form 1040 line 27a and Schedule 3 line 11, and compare that result with the 15% earned-income amount.
