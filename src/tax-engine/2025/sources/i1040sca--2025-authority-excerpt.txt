Medical and Dental Expenses, line 3: multiply Form 1040 or 1040-SR line 11b by 7.5% (0.075).

State and Local Tax Deduction, line 5e: if Form 1040 or 1040-SR line 11b is $500,000 or less ($250,000 if married filing separately), enter the smaller of line 5d or $40,000 ($20,000 if married filing separately). Above that threshold, use the State and Local Tax Deduction Worksheet.

State and Local Tax Deduction Worksheet: start with $40,000; subtract $500,000 ($250,000 if married filing separately) from modified income; multiply the excess by 30%; subtract that amount from $40,000; use not less than $10,000; then enter the smaller of that result (half of that result if married filing separately) or Schedule A line 5d. If line 5d is $10,000 ($5,000 if married filing separately) or less, enter line 5d without completing the worksheet.
