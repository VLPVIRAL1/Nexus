# Tax engine

The 2025 research engine is pure, deterministic, year-versioned, decimal-based, and independent from UI and PDF rendering. It implements all five filing-status tax methods and standard deductions, reviewed dependents, Schedule A, Schedule 8812 CTC/ODC/ACTC, and supported portions of Schedules B, C, D, E, F and SE, Schedules 1 and 2, Forms 8949/8889, simplified Form 8995, the qualified-dividend/capital-gain worksheet, and Form 1040. Withholding follows the active-source payment path and Schedule SE aggregates Schedule C/F profit by owner before applying each person's Social Security wage interaction.

The expanded review profile supports simple basis-known 1099-B transactions without adjustments (capital losses over $3,000 block for carryover handling), code-7 1099-R distributions with a determined taxable amount, 1099-G unemployment compensation, 1099-K gross receipts only after exact accepted Schedule C reconciliation, and code-1 HSA distributions whose reviewed qualified medical expenses equal the gross distribution. Every other code, adjustment or nonzero special-treatment field is retained and blocks calculation.

Schedule C supports cash-method nonnegative activities with mapped plus separately reconciled receipts, returns, COGS, other income and line-category expenses. Schedule E supports simple Part I rental/royalty activities after at-risk, passive, depreciation and QBI review. Schedule F supports cash-method nonnegative farm activities with reviewed crop-insurance, SE and QBI treatment. Losses and linked calculations such as vehicle, home office, passive/basis limitations, Parts II–IV, accrual farming and other special elections block the result instead of being guessed.

Schedule A applies the 7.5% medical floor, 2025 SALT cap and modified-AGI phaseout, MFS spouse-itemization rule, and only preparer-reviewed mortgage, charity, eligible disaster-loss and other amounts. Schedule 8812 applies the $2,200 CTC, $500 ODC, filing-status phaseout, $1,700-per-child ACTC limit, earned-income method and three-child payroll-tax alternative. Claimable-taxpayer, age/blindness additions, EITC, Form 2441 and other unimplemented treatments remain explicit blockers rather than inferred zeroes.

`src/tax-engine/2025/rule-package.ts` pins immutable IRS URLs, retrieval dates, archived content hashes, the February 2026 Form 8995 correction, and fixture identifiers. Runtime calculations use the committed 2,062-band IRS tax table below $100,000 and the published computation worksheet at and above $100,000. They never fetch mutable tax data. Trace steps record operands, form lines, worksheet choice, rounding, rule ID, and result. Dependency traversal detects cycles and returns explicit blockers for incomplete or unsupported paths.

The package status is `research_unapproved`. Calculation results are draft research previews and carry a review warning. Activation for Reviewed Draft requires an independent qualified tax-professional review, named reviewer, independently checked fixtures, and a new immutable approved package version.
