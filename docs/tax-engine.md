# Tax engine

The 2025 research engine is pure, deterministic, year-versioned, decimal-based, and independent from UI and PDF rendering. It implements all five filing-status tax methods, the dependent-taxpayer standard-deduction worksheet, reviewed person-specific age/blindness additions, the 2025 Social Security Benefits Worksheet, reviewed dependents, Schedule A, Schedule 8812 CTC/ODC/ACTC, EITC and Schedule EIC, child/dependent-care credit and Form 2441, AOTC/LLC and Form 8863, Form 6251 AMT, paid-preparer Form 8867 due diligence, annual Form 8962 Premium Tax Credit reconciliation, Schedule 3, and supported portions of Schedules B, C, D, E, F and SE, Schedules 1 and 2, Forms 8949/8889, simplified Form 8995, the qualified-dividend/capital-gain worksheet, and Form 1040. A reviewed case requiring Form 8615 remains explicitly blocked. Withholding and evidence-backed estimated, extension and prior-year credit-elect payments follow the active payment path; Schedule SE aggregates Schedule C/F profit by owner before applying each person's Social Security wage interaction.

The expanded review profile supports simple basis-known 1099-B transactions without adjustments (capital losses over $3,000 block for carryover handling), code-7 1099-R distributions with a determined taxable amount, 1099-G unemployment compensation, 1099-K gross receipts only after exact accepted Schedule C reconciliation, and code-1 HSA distributions whose reviewed qualified medical expenses equal the gross distribution. Every other code, adjustment or nonzero special-treatment field is retained and blocks calculation.

Schedule C supports cash-method nonnegative activities with mapped plus separately reconciled receipts, returns, COGS, other income and line-category expenses. Schedule E supports simple Part I rental/royalty activities after at-risk, passive, depreciation and QBI review. Schedule F supports cash-method nonnegative farm activities with reviewed crop-insurance, SE and QBI treatment. Losses and linked calculations such as vehicle, home office, passive/basis limitations, Parts II–IV, accrual farming and other special elections block the result instead of being guessed.

Intake support is conditional rather than merely declarative: an affirmative Marketplace, non-withholding-payment, supported Schedule E/F, claimable-taxpayer, or age/blindness answer must reconcile to the corresponding persisted profile, activity, or reviewed fact set, and a negative answer cannot coexist with that data. Partnership, S corporation, estate, trust and REMIC applicability is asked separately and remains blocking until the Schedule E Parts II–IV expansion is implemented.

SSA-1099 and RRB-1099 tier-1-equivalent benefit statements are typed source records shared by manual entry and canonical JSON. The worksheet applies the 2025 single/HOH/QSS and MFJ base amounts and 50%/85% tiers, adds taxable benefits to Form 1040 line 6b, and includes statement withholding in the active withholding ledger. Statement reconciliation failures, prior-year lump-sum elections, RRB workers' compensation offsets, all MFS cases pending a living-apart fact module, and IRA/student-loan deduction interactions block rather than silently estimate.

The Form 6251 slice uses the 2025 filing-status exemption, phaseout and 26/28-percent brackets; adds back the standard deduction or reviewed Schedule A taxes; accepts enumerated private-activity-bond, ISO, depreciation, passive-activity, loss-limitation and other nonnegative adjustments; recalculates the AMT QBI limitation; and preserves preferential-income rates. It flows additional AMT through Schedule 2 Part I and Form 1040 line 17. Dependent-child exemption worksheets, Form 8801 and unrepresented or negative adjustment cases remain explicit blockers.

Schedule A applies the 7.5% medical floor, 2025 SALT cap and modified-AGI phaseout, MFS spouse-itemization rule, and only preparer-reviewed mortgage, charity, eligible disaster-loss and other amounts. Schedule 8812 applies the $2,200 CTC, $500 ODC, filing-status phaseout, $1,700-per-child ACTC limit, earned-income method and three-child payroll-tax alternative. The EITC module uses reviewed SSN-eligibility facts, qualifying-child tests, age, investment-income and filing-status limits. Form 2441 applies reconciled provider/person expenses, earned-income and employer-benefit limits. Form 8863 applies reviewed student eligibility, adjusted expenses, 2025 phaseouts and the AOTC refundable split. Cases outside each stated profile produce named blockers rather than inferred zeroes.

`src/tax-engine/2025/rule-package.ts` pins immutable IRS URLs, retrieval dates, archived content hashes, the February 2026 Form 8995 correction, and fixture identifiers. Runtime calculations use the committed 2,062-band IRS tax table below $100,000 and the published computation worksheet at and above $100,000. They never fetch mutable tax data. Trace steps record operands, form lines, worksheet choice, rounding, rule ID, and result. Dependency traversal detects cycles and returns explicit blockers for incomplete or unsupported paths.

The package status is `research_unapproved`. Calculation results are draft research previews and carry a review warning. Activation for Reviewed Draft requires an independent qualified tax-professional review, named reviewer, independently checked fixtures, and a new immutable approved package version.
