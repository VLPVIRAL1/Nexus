# Form rendering

The calculation engine produces authoritative form data objects. `form-data-2025.ts` converts those objects into a year-specific rendering plan, and `tax-form-renderer.ts` renders the plan without performing tax arithmetic.

The current renderer is a controlled review renderer, not an approved filing renderer. It renders Form 1040, including Social Security benefit lines 6a/6b and AMT line 17, and dependent classifications; the supported portions of Schedules 1, 2, 3, A, B, C, D, E, F, EIC and SE; Schedule 8812; Forms 2441, 6251, 8863, 8867, 8889, 8949, 8962 and 8995; and the qualified-dividend/capital-gain and Social Security benefit computation worksheets when required. For archived Form 1040, Schedule 1/2/B/C/SE and Form 8995 templates it verifies the official IRS PDF hash, includes the untouched official artwork with a prominent review watermark, and appends the controlled calculated-value pages. Other implemented forms use the controlled internal layout. Every page remains `DRAFT — NOT FOR FILING`.

The package begins with a run/revision/version cover and required/rendered/missing-form manifest. It records internal-draft or client-copy mode, page-level identity validation, template status and hard-disabled filing-copy/barcode/e-file flags. Missing forms or identifying fields remain visible and make the package incomplete. Populating approved IRS coordinates, removing the draft watermark or enabling filing remains a separate, independently reviewed release gate.
